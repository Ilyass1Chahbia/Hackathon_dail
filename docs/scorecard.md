# Your demo checklist

Use these prompts to prepare your presentation. Show evidence from your prototype, client conversation and response to feedback.


## Speed



- Defines a narrow outcome after the first interview.

- Makes a usable first workflow available at the first review.

- Prioritises the riskiest assumption rather than polishing a peripheral screen.

- Integrates a meaningful client correction within the working window.

- Leaves a prototype another person can open and a usable handoff.



## Creativity



- Reframes the client's initial request using something learned in the interview.

- Considers a materially different intervention before choosing one.

- Combines context, interaction or tools in a way that reduces the user's work.

- Makes the proposed workflow fit the actual actor and their constraints.

- Uses the changed information to improve the approach, not merely its appearance.



## Salesmanship



- Listens for consequences and checks understanding with the client.

- Explains the benefit in the client's operational language.

- Demonstrates the result clearly enough for the client to react to it.

- Answers an objection with evidence, a limitation or a proposed test.

- Ends with a specific next validation step the client can understand.



## Confidence



- Makes a reasoned scope choice under uncertainty.

- States assumptions and distinguishes them from established facts.

- Changes position when stronger evidence appears.

- Handles a failure or unknown without bluffing or concealing it.

- Defends the boundary of what the prototype can responsibly do.



## Innovation



- Proposes a useful workflow change beyond restating existing information.

- Demonstrates an event causing a relevant next-step proposal.

- Connects the proposal to inspectable evidence.

- Makes human approval or correction part of the interaction.

- Identifies how to test the idea's value with the next real case.


Finish with what works, what is simulated, what you learned from the client, and the next real case you would test.
